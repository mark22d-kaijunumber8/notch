Notch

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Notch does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no server to send anything to, and the app makes no network connections of any kind. Everything it knows about you is stored on your iPhone and stays there.

Nobody — including the developer — can see your training, your weight, your food or your photos.

What the app stores on your device

All of this lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • Your training history — routines, exercises, sets, weights, reps, reps-in-reserve, session dates and durations
  • Your body measurements — the ones you type in yourself
  • Your progress photos — if you choose to take any
  • Your settings — nutrition phase, target rate, theme and units

If you delete Notch, iOS deletes all of it with the app.

Apple Health

Notch asks your permission to read the following from Apple Health, so it can show your weight trend and your nutrition alongside your training:

  • Body weight, body fat percentage and lean body mass
  • Dietary energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat
  • Heart rate, used only to shade the training calendar by how hard a session was
  • Date of birth, used only to estimate your maximum heart rate
  • Workouts

It writes one kind of data back to Apple Health: completed strength workouts, so your training shows up in your activity rings and in other apps you use.

Granting this access is optional. Notch works without it — the screens that would show Health data display clearly-labelled example figures instead, behind a banner saying so. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app, under Sharing → Apps.

Health data read by Notch is used only to show you your own information inside the app. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with anyone, and never leaves your device.

Camera

Notch asks for camera access only when you choose to take a progress photo. Photos are stored inside the app on your device. They are not uploaded, not backed up to any service by the app, and not shared with anyone.

What Notch does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-ups or logins
  • No servers, no cloud sync, no backups to anywhere
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs — none at all
  • No data sold or shared with anyone, for any purpose

Children

Notch is a strength-training app intended for general audiences. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Your rights

Because your data never leaves your iPhone, there is nothing held about you anywhere for anyone to disclose, correct or delete. You remain in full control of it:

  • To export it, use your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup)
  • To delete it, delete the app — iOS removes all of its data with it
  • To stop Health access, revoke it in the Health app under Sharing → Apps

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. If a future version of Notch ever collected data — it does not today — this policy would be updated before that version was released, and the app would ask your permission first.
