Common questions

My weight and calories aren't showing up

Notch reads both from Apple Health. Open the Health app, go to Sharing → Apps → Notch, and check the switches are on. If a screen shows a "Showing example data" banner, Health has not returned anything yet — those figures are generated, not yours.

MyFitnessPal only writes nutrition to Apple Health for Premium subscribers. Weight from a smart scale syncs regardless.

I tapped "Grant access" and nothing happened

iOS shows its Health permission sheet only once per app. After that, the only place access can be changed is the Health app, under Sharing → Apps → Notch.

Why is it asking me to lift more than I managed last time?

Each session is built from your last one. If you logged more reps than your target at the reps-in-reserve you entered, the next session adds load. Logging your reps-in-reserve honestly is what keeps those numbers sensible.

How do I change program?

Programs tab → pick one → "Add to my routines". Your existing routines stay where they are; nothing is overwritten.

Where is my data, and how do I back it up?

Everything is stored on your iPhone and nowhere else — there is no account and no cloud sync. An iPhone backup (iCloud or to a computer) includes it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
